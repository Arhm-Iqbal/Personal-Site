I’m an Honours Neuroscience student at the University of Alberta and technical co-founder of SimuPrep, a platform for clinical simulation preparation. My research experience includes neurovascular clinical trials and experimental neuroscience.

Work

  • 2025–present

    SimuPrep

    Co-founded a platform for clinical simulation preparation, now being licensed by NAIT. The project won first place and $5,000 at natHacks 2025. Exhibited SimuPrep in the ALPHA startup program at Web Summit Vancouver.

  • 2026–present

    Wi-Fi Biosensing

    Leading a project with the Undergraduate Artificial Intelligence Society to investigate respiration, movement, and sleep monitoring through Wi-Fi signals. Uses ESP32 boards, signal processing, and machine learning to explore sensing without wearables.

  • 2026

    Evidence Poverty Auditor

    Built a biomedical knowledge-graph pipeline to examine evidence gaps in AI drug-discovery models for neglected tropical diseases. Won the RBC Poster Competition at the STEM Fellowship Inter-University Big Data Challenge. Code

  • 2026

    Phone-based motion tracking

    Developed a system that uses two phones and a transformer model to estimate hip, knee, and ankle angles for rehabilitation monitoring outside the lab. Second place, Problem Provider Division, natHacks 2026. Recorder code

  • 2026

    Basirah

    Built a platform for incident reporting and community support for Muslims facing hate in Canada, with a focus on privacy. Developed at The Harvest Anti-Muslim Hate Hackathon. Live app and code

  • 2026

    GrantPath

    Developed a tool to guide students through disability-related funding applications. Second place and $3,000 in prizes at natIgnite 2026.

  • 2025

    Vibe-Maxxing

    Computer vision project that won first place at the UAIS Sillycon Hackathon. Devpost

  • 2025

    Drosophila temperature-control system

    Built the control software and thermal hardware for a Raspberry Pi system used in Drosophila memory-suppression experiments. The system controls temperature to activate or inhibit temperature-sensitive neural circuits.
